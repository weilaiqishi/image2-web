LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy & Cookies

The Image2 desktop app and this marketing site are separate data boundaries. This page explains what each one processes and what choices you have if display advertising is enabled.

Last updated: July 19, 2026

The desktop app is separate from this site

The Image2 Studio desktop app does not submit your API key to this marketing site. The API key is kept in your operating system credential store; conversations, tasks, images, and annotations are stored locally. When the app generates or edits an image, it sends the request directly to the OpenAI-compatible gateway you configured, so that gateway's and its upstream providers' privacy terms also apply.

This marketing site does not accept API keys, provide online generation, or read conversations and images from the desktop app.

Technical data needed to deliver the site

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver pages, protect the service, and diagnose faults, hosting and network services may process IP addresses, request times, requested URLs, browser or device information, and security-event data. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Google AdSense and your choice

This site may use Google AdSense to display ads. Before you make a choice, this site does not load the Google AdSense ad tag or request ads. This site's advertising privacy control records your choice only in local browser storage.

If you consent, this site requests non-personalized ads only. Even so, Google and participating ad technology providers may use cookies or similar technologies and process IP addresses, device and browser information, current-page information, and ad-impression and interaction data for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you decline, this site does not request display ads. Declining ads does not prevent you from reading the site or downloading Image2 from GitHub.

Cookies, local storage, and consent records

This site's advertising privacy control uses local storage to remember your choice. Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies after it is enabled. Beyond data needed to deliver the site, remember your privacy choice, and display ads after consent, this site does not require an account or receive image-generation content.

Manage or withdraw consent

You can reopen this site's advertising privacy choices at any time. If ads have already loaded, withdrawing consent reloads the page to stop future ad requests; it cannot undo data Google has already processed. You can also manage Google's personalized-ad settings in My Ad Center.

External services

This site links to third-party services including GitHub, Google, and Cloudflare. Their own terms and privacy policies apply after you leave this site. Do not post API keys, access tokens, or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

Policy updates and contact

If the site's data practices change materially, this page will be updated with revised content and a new date. For questions about this policy or the Image2 project, contact the maintainers through GitHub Issues.
