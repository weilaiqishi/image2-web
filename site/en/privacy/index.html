Privacy & Cookies

The Image2 desktop app and this marketing site are separate data boundaries. This page explains what each one processes and what choices you have if display advertising is enabled.

Last updated: August 5, 2026

The desktop app is separate from this site

The Image2 Studio desktop app does not submit your API key to this marketing site. The API key is kept in your operating system credential store; conversations, tasks, images, and annotations are stored locally. When the app generates or edits an image, it sends the request directly to the OpenAI-compatible gateway you configured, so that gateway's and its upstream providers' privacy terms also apply.

This marketing site does not accept API keys, provide online generation, or read conversations and images from the desktop app.

Technical data needed to deliver the site

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver pages, protect the service, and diagnose faults, hosting and network services may process IP addresses, request times, requested URLs, browser or device information, and security-event data. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Advertising providers and your choice

The advertising provider enabled in this build is: __AD_PROVIDER__. A build selects at most one provider; Google AdSense and Adsterra are never loaded together in the same ad unit. Their loading behavior differs: Google AdSense loads only after explicit consent. When Adsterra is enabled, the fixed approved tag appears in 8 existing ad slots across the Chinese and English home, guide, cases, and Codex troubleshooting pages, and loads immediately as the page is parsed without a prior consent prompt.

When Google AdSense is selected, this site requests non-personalized ads only. Even so, Google and participating ad technology providers may use cookies or similar technologies and process IP addresses, device and browser information, current-page information, and ad-impression and interaction data for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

When Adsterra is selected, this site permits only a standard Display Banner for an approved website and placement. It does not integrate Popunder, Push, Direct Link, or Social Bar formats. Adsterra's policies state that it may process technical data such as IP address, browser type and version, time zone and approximate location, operating system and platform, visited URLs, page interactions, and advertising details, and may use cookies or similar technologies. See the Adsterra Privacy Policy and Adsterra Publisher Terms.

The Google AdSense consent interface allows you to reject loading; rejection does not prevent you from reading site content or downloading Image2 from GitHub. The production Adsterra build does not offer this pre-load choice.

Cookies, local storage, and consent records

The Google AdSense privacy control uses local storage to remember your choice. The Adsterra tag does not use this site's local storage as a loading gate; it and the advertising resources it loads may use cookies or similar technologies. Beyond data needed to deliver the site, support AdSense privacy choices, and display ads, this site does not require an account or receive image-generation content.

Advertising privacy choices

When Google AdSense is used, the page provides a consent interface before loading, and you can also manage Google's personalized-ad settings in My Ad Center. When Adsterra is enabled, this consent or reset control is not shown. Leaving the troubleshooting article prevents later loads of that page, but cannot undo data the advertising provider has already processed.

External services

This site links to third-party services including GitHub, Google, and Cloudflare. Their own terms and privacy policies apply after you leave this site. Do not post API keys, access tokens, or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

Policy updates and contact

If the site's data practices change materially, this page will be updated with revised content and a new date. For questions about this policy or the Image2 project, contact the maintainers through GitHub Issues.
