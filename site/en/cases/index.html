GENERATION LEDGER / 001-008

Eight original generation cases

These are not stock samples. Each case records the task type, composition, constraints, and review criteria used to turn a loose direction into an image task that can enter the serial queue.

PortraitProductFoodInterfaceSpaceCharacterIllustrationStoryboard

PORTRAIT / 01

Blue-hour arcade

The target is credible commercial fashion photography, not a retouched beauty poster. The task binds natural skin, wet reflections, restrained future clothing, and cool environmental light.

Original East Asian creative professional beneath a rain-soaked concrete arcade.
Vertical three-quarter frame, natural skin, charcoal technical jacket,
cyan practical light and one warm rim. No brand, copy, logo, or watermark.
CHECK
Original identity, textured skin, coherent light across subject and pavement.
AVOID
Generic beauty face, plastic skin, branded clothes, broken hands, decorative copy.

PRODUCT / 02

Graphite audio object

Manufacturable structure and material come before campaign lighting. The model must resolve vents, controls, and edges rather than return an unexplained black box.

Fictional premium wireless speaker, matte graphite enclosure, dark mineral plinth.
Precise machined vents and tactile controls, strip-light studio setup,
cyan edge highlight. No brand name, readable copy, logo, or watermark.
CHECK
Buildable structure, distinct materials, readable edges in the dark finish.
AVOID
Random controls, floating parts, fake brands, and decorative glowing seams.

EDITORIAL / 03

Midnight noodle bar

Food, gesture, and human relationships must all hold together. The task defines the serving action, the guests' attention, and the balance between warm task light and cool street light.

Evening documentary scene in a compact modern noodle bar.
A chef plates a vivid bowl while two friends lean in naturally.
Real food and hands; no restaurant branding, menu copy, logo, or watermark.
CHECK
Coherent attention and gesture, grounded bowls, food that still feels hot.
AVOID
Staged smiles, repeated fingers, garbled menus, fictional chain branding.

INTERFACE / 04

Creative task console

The interface must look shippable before it looks cinematic. The task names the device hierarchy, image queue, reference thumbnails, and status signals while excluding known product UI.

One tablet and one phone show a fictional creative task application:
dark image queue, reference thumbnails, clear controls, and cyan status marks.
Practical hierarchy; no known brand UI, logo, or watermark.
CHECK
Consistent device perspective, useful density, believable controls and states.
AVOID
Concept HUDs, meaningless charts, brand icons, glow, and unreadable glyphs.

SPACE / 05

Cold-coast studio

Buildability anchors the image. Blackened timber, glass, coastal weather, and warm interiors form one material system, while impossible cantilevers are explicitly excluded.

Buildable blackened-timber and glass studio above a cold ocean.
Overcast dawn, light mist, warm interior work surfaces visible through the facade.
No impossible cantilever, copy, logo, or watermark.
CHECK
Credible structure, consistent scale, reflections that retain interior depth.
AVOID
Floating volumes, buildings without entry, repeated glazing, excessive fog.

CHARACTER / 06

Orbital bay engineer

Profession and tools lead the character direction instead of a borrowed franchise. A compact utility suit, maintenance bay, and helmet at the hip communicate the role without superhero armor.

Original engineer in a weathered orbital maintenance bay.
Compact modular utility suit, helmet held at the hip, industrial realism.
No franchise reference, weapon, heroic armor, copy, logo, or watermark.
CHECK
Job-specific equipment, coherent hands, matching wear across suit and space.
AVOID
Franchise silhouettes, weapons, floating gear, nonfunctional armor.

ILLUSTRATION / 07

The glass whale archive

The surreal premise stays mature through material constraints: ink, cut paper, and luminous glass each carry a distinct role instead of relying on a children's-cartoon character.

Translucent glass whale floating through a night archive,
paper constellations and shelves bending gently into the distance.
Refined ink, cut paper, luminous glass; no copy, logo, watermark, or cartoon styling.
CHECK
Distinct paper, ink, and glass; light connects the whale to the room.
AVOID
Storybook faces, neon overload, random spine text, plastic 3D material.

STORYBOARD / 08

First train, four frames

A storyboard is not four unrelated images. The task locks one train, platform, time, and camera language, then gives each panel a distinct arrival, door, passenger, and departure beat.

Four-frame film contact sheet of the same electric train at the same foggy station:
wide arrival, door detail, lone passenger silhouette, train leaving into mist.
Exact 2×2 grid, continuous scene, no caption, copy, logo, or watermark.
CHECK
Exactly four panels, continuous train and station, a complete action sequence.
AVOID
Extra panels, location jumps, changing train design, captions, color drift.
